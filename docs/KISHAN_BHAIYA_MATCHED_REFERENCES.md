## 2. Strongest research references for the problem statement

### A. Odisha production and market-access need

Odisha produced 150 lakh MT of foodgrain, 111.02 lakh MT of vegetables, and 27.2 lakh MT of fruit in 2024–25. Small holdings and limited marketable surplus continue to restrict market access.

**Use for:** problem size, Odisha relevance, FPO and market-access justification.

- https://finance.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-08/OES%202025-26%20Highlights%20and%20Executive%20Summary%20-English.pdf
- https://finance.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/Economic%20Survey%202024-25%20-Full%20Document_1.pdf

### B. Post-harvest loss and surplus rescue

NABCONS estimates losses of 6.02–15.05% for fruits and 4.87–11.61% for vegetables. Odisha estimates include 13.19% for tomato and 13.64% for mushroom.

**Use for:** FEFO, expiry alerts, faster logistics, cold-chain flags, and surplus-rescue justification.

- https://www.mofpi.gov.in/sites/default/files/phl_study_final_report_07.12.2022_2.pdf
- https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2151371&lang=2&reg=48

### C. Why matching must include transport

Research links better transport and market information with higher farmer price realisation.

**Use for:** matching plus logistics, net-realisation calculation, and route-planning justification.

- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306919218300320

### D. Why FPO workflows matter

Research shows that farmer organisations can improve market access, bargaining power, income, and product quality.

**Use for:** FPO membership, aggregation, member contribution, and settlement transparency.

- https://www.nature.com/articles/s43016-020-00164-x
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-05063-9

### E. Why “digital marketplace” alone is insufficient

Odisha’s e-NAM results were mixed, showing that digital listings need training, quality control, logistics, and reliable settlement.

**Use for:** explaining why KISHAN BHAIYA includes fulfilment, quality, logistics, payment, training, and dispute workflows instead of only listings.

- https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/AERR/article/view/140377
- https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/2277975218807277

### F. Why traceability must cover the complete chain

FAO and research reviews support farm-to-market data records for traceability, transparency, recall, and trust.

**Use for:** unique lot IDs, harvest/quality data, reservation history, pickup/delivery proof, and audit events.

- https://www.fao.org/family-farming/detail/en/c/1651890/
- https://www.mdpi.com/2077-0472/13/9/1757
- https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/23/11/5342

## 4. Recommended citation paragraph

Use this paragraph in the report:

> Odisha’s agricultural output and transition toward high-value horticulture create a substantial requirement for efficient aggregation and market linkage. The Odisha Economic Survey identifies small holdings and low marketable surplus as continuing constraints, while the Government of India’s NABCONS study reports fruit and vegetable post-harvest losses reaching 15.05% and 11.61%, respectively. Research on Indian market channels further shows that market information produces the greatest benefit when combined with transport access. Studies of farmer organisations support aggregation and market-linkage services, but Odisha’s mixed e-NAM outcomes demonstrate that digitising trade alone is insufficient. These findings support KISHAN BHAIYA’s integrated approach combining verified lots, FPO aggregation, buyer matching, freshness-aware reservation, consolidated logistics, settlement visibility, and dispute evidence.

## 6. Final novelty statement supported by these references

> KISHAN BHAIYA does not claim to invent digital agricultural commerce. It integrates proven elements from e-NAM-style trading, FPO aggregation, managed fresh-produce supply chains, transport discovery, demand-led sourcing, and lot traceability into an Odisha-focused workflow. Its proposed novelty lies in the connected execution of retail and institutional demand through verified lots, transparent multi-seller reservation, pre-harvest conversion, FEFO allocation, seller-approved surplus rescue, capacity-aware consolidated logistics, member-level FPO settlement, and auditable exception recovery.
